# Wish You Were Here

A dreamcore browser game: four little planets joined by walkable bridges. Wander, touch things, and find all 12 memories.

Play: https://khyatinagaria.github.io/wish-you-were-here/

Controls: WASD to walk, mouse to look, space to jump, shift to run, E to touch things, M for the road map, F for fullscreen.

Music: Neon Grid City plays "Home" by Resonance (slowed + reverb edit); World 1-1 plays "Agitations tropicales" by L'Impératrice (karaoke version). All rights belong to the artists.
